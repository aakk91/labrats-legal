Start with what you want to understand. These guides connect a question to a task, a measurement and a record you can revisit.

01 / Reaction time

Reaction Time Test & Daily Tracking for iPhone

A reaction time test records the delay between a signal and your response. LabRats for iPhone saves repeated runs so you can follow your own timing across days, alongside the context you choose to record.

↗02 / Memory

Memory Tests for iPhone: Digit Span, Corsi & N-back

A memory test can ask you to hold a sequence, remember positions or update information as new items arrive. LabRats includes different tasks for those demands and keeps their results separate so you can follow each one.

↗03 / Attention & focus

Attention & Focus Tests: Stroop, Go/No-Go & N-back

Attention games place different demands on you: wait for the right signal, ignore a distraction or keep updating what matters. LabRats records the responses behind those tasks so you can examine timing and errors together.

↗04 / Supplement journal

Supplement Tracker & Journal with Cognitive Game Results

A supplement tracker is most useful when you can revisit the context around an entry. LabRats brings a supplement log, a private Journal and saved cognitive-game results into the same app for iPhone.

↗05 / Nootropic tracking

Nootropic Tracker: Journal, Memory & Reaction-Time Results

If you are looking for a nootropic tracker, start with a clear distinction: recording what you took is different from showing what caused a change. LabRats keeps a personal log alongside cognitive-game results so you can look at the record in detail.

↗06 / Personal experiments

Personal Experiment Tracker & N-of-1 Journal for iPhone

A personal experiment tracker helps you define what you want to compare before you collect the result. LabRats lets you choose a question, select games, keep a schedule and read your own saved measurements alongside your Journal.

↗07 / Brain games

Brain Training App for iPhone: 30 Games & Personal Trends

LabRats is a brain-game and personal tracking app for iPhone. Its 30 Practice games cover different demands, including reaction time, memory, attention, reasoning, spatial tasks and multitasking. Saved results help you look beyond a single score.
