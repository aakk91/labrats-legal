01 / Nootropic tracking

Nootropic Tracker App & Personal Journal

LabRats is a nootropic tracker app for iPhone that connects a personal log with saved cognitive-game results. Record your own product names and timing, keep notes about the day, and use a planned comparison when you have a specific question.

↗02 / Peptide journal

Peptide Tracker & Journal for iPhone

LabRats lets you keep a peptide tracking journal alongside nootropics, supplements and saved cognitive-game results. Enter your own product names, record when an entry happened and keep the day’s notes and measurements together on iPhone.

↗03 / Supplement journal

Supplement Tracker App with Cognitive Game Results

LabRats is a supplement tracker and personal journal for iPhone, with 30 cognitive games alongside your entries. Record what you take, keep the day’s context and revisit reaction time, memory and attention results in the same app.

↗04 / Personal experiments

Personal Experiment Tracker & N-of-1 Journal

LabRats is a personal experiment tracker for iPhone with selected cognitive games, phase schedules and a Journal. Connect a question about your recorded routine, supplements or nootropics to measurements you can read in their own units.

↗05 / Brain games

Brain Games App for iPhone: 30 Cognitive Games

LabRats is a brain games app for iPhone with 30 Practice games and saved personal results. Use it for short task practice, or keep the results alongside a nootropic, peptide or supplement journal and a planned personal comparison.

↗06 / Reaction time

Reaction Time Tracker App for iPhone

LabRats is a reaction time tracker app for iPhone that keeps your saved response times, correct answers and daily context together. Use the same task across days, then revisit your personal record instead of keeping only your fastest tap.

↗07 / Memory

Memory Test App: Digit Span, Corsi & N-back

LabRats brings digit span, Corsi blocks and N-back memory tests to one iPhone app. Choose the demand you want to follow, save repeated runs and compare results from the same task format.

↗08 / Attention & focus

Attention Test App: Stroop & Go/No-Go

LabRats is an iPhone app with attention tasks including Stroop, Flanker and Go/No-Go. Save your runs and read response time alongside accuracy, rather than treating one number as your overall level of focus.
